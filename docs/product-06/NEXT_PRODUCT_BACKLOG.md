| 1 | **Selection policy review (Rust, behaviour change — needs its own decision).** Today the best *current* answer wins by presence rank (`present` > `absent` > `unknown`), then by newest id, regardless of how specific it is. So a computer-wide `present` beats a project-specific `absent`, and a `local-cli` row can beat an exact-surface row. PRODUCT-06 only *discloses* this; it does not change it. Decide whether a project-specific or exact-surface answer should take precedence, or whether the status should become "mixed evidence". | A project that explicitly found the tool absent is no longer told "present" on the strength of a computer-wide name match. | A product decision, then a `classify_request` change with a migration-free, additive status or a documented precedence, and new Rust tests. ## Order and why
1 first: the origin display makes the precedence question visible; a user can now see a computer-wide `present` winning over a project-specific `absent`, and the product should decide whether that is intended. 2 follows 1. 3 is the other Rust fix. 4 only after 1 (an acknowledgement should be bound to a precise fact). 5 is the first user-driven write of its kind. 6 can happen at any time and does not depend on code. 7 is small and independent.
